Exposome Maps

A catalogue of exposome datasets: environmental exposures such as air pollution, green space, temperature and the food environment. It includes the data used in the ExposomeNL and EXPANSE projects at Utrecht University, as well as datasets published by other organisations. For background on our conceptualisation of the exposome and its categorisation into four dimensions, see these openly accessible publications:

  • The exposome and health: Where chemistry meets biology
  • Developing the building blocks to elucidate the impact of the urban exposome on cardiometabolic-pulmonary disease

Where to get the data

  • UU · On request Made available by Utrecht University (IRAS). Access is granted on request; see the dataset page for how to apply.
  • External Published and maintained by another organisation. Each dataset links to the provider, where you can access it under the provider's own terms.

Using the map

  • Pick a layer in the panel on the right; it is shown straight away for its most recent date. A coloured dot shows where each dataset comes from; greyed-out datasets are not available on the map.
  • Use the date control (or the arrows) to change the date, and the slider to change transparency.
  • Click anywhere on the map to see the value at that location.
  • Data were reprojected from their original coordinate reference system for display, so values on the map may differ slightly from the source data because of interpolation.

About this app

Exposome Maps is built and maintained by the Data Management team at the Institute for Risk Assessment Sciences (IRAS), Utrecht University. Questions about the app can be sent to expanse@uu.nl, or open an issue on GitHub.
